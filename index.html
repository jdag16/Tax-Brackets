Employer CPP matches the employee's CPP and CPP2. Employer EI is 1.4 times the employee premium. EHT (1.95% applies once BC payroll is over $1.5M), WorkSafeBC (varies by industry, find yours on your WorkSafeBC rate notice) and pay-linked benefits are charged on wages plus vacation pay. Flat-premium benefits like health and dental don't change with a raise, so they are left out. CPP and EI are calculated on base wages; WorkSafeBC's maximum assessable earnings cap is not applied.
